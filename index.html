Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Implant placement is performed under local anesthesia and is completely painless in most cases. Any minor discomfort or swelling in the days following can be easily managed with prescribed aftercare. Our clinical team schedules attentive follow-ups to support every stage of healing.
Treatment duration depends on case complexity, but typically ranges between 6 and 18 months. During your first visit, we design a detailed treatment plan featuring a digital 3D preview of your final smile.
We typically recommend a professional cleaning every 6 months, though intervals may vary based on periodontal condition, implants, orthodontic appliances, and personal lifestyle. We provide personalized guidance during your examination.
Your first visit is dedicated to comprehensive assessment and understanding your goals. It includes a thorough clinical exam, digital diagnostics as needed, and an in-depth review of treatment options with transparent, no-obligation pricing.
